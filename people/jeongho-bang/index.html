About

Professor JeongHo Bang leads AQua.CAI at Yonsei University. His research spans quantum information theory, quantum computation, and machine learning, with work on quantum learning methods, information-theoretic constraints, and quantum protocols.

His research career includes appointments at the Korea Institute for Advanced Study, the Electronics and Telecommunications Research Institute, and university research groups in quantum information and control.
